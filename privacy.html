How your information is kept private

The short version: your business belongs to you and the people you invite. Nobody else can open it from inside BusyNes.

Who can see what

  • Your business is separate. Everything you add (products, stock, sales, orders, messages, who owns what) belongs to your business. Other businesses on BusyNes cannot see it, and you cannot see theirs.
  • Only people you invite. A person only joins your business if you invite their email address and they sign up with it. You choose what they can do: partner (owns part of the business), staff (records sales, orders and stock) or delivery (sees and takes orders only).
  • Inside your business, everyone can see what was sold and when. Money details (amounts, credit, who owes what, loan fees) are shown only to the people who handled that sale and, if you allow it in settings, to owners and partners.
  • No admin screen. BusyNes has no page where anyone, including the person who built it, can browse businesses or look through other people's sales.
  • These rules are enforced by the database itself, not just hidden on screen, so they hold even if someone tries to bypass the app.

What we keep

  • Your email address and name, so you can sign in and so partners know who you are.
  • Your phone number, only if you add it (so the people in your business can call you).
  • What you record in BusyNes: products, prices, stock, sales, orders, payments, messages.
  • If you turn on phone notifications, the address your phone gives us to send them.

Your password is handled by the sign-in service and is never visible to us.

Being straight about the limits

BusyNes stores its data in a hosted database. Whoever administers that database can technically reach the stored data, as with almost any app, and so can the hosting company. BusyNes is built so that the person who runs it does not browse your business, and it has no feature that lets them. But this is a promise from a small project, not a technical impossibility. If you would not be comfortable with that, please don't enter information you consider highly sensitive.

This is a test version

BusyNes is being tested with a few people and is still being improved. Features may change and bugs will happen. Keep your own record of anything important until you're comfortable relying on it. Your feedback is very welcome.

Your control

  • The owner can remove anyone from a business at any time. They lose access straight away.
  • The owner can delete the whole business. Everything in it is removed for good.
  • You can leave a business you were invited to. Your past sales stay in its records, because they belong to the business's accounts.
  • To have your login and details removed completely, ask the person who shared BusyNes with you.
